While creating my [Pinterest mood board](/desk/office_mood_board) for my home office, I found a lot of inspiration for **mossariums**: moss terrariums, also called a mini rainforest or a micro landscape.
I wanted a small piece of nature on my desk, but without the care of a normal houseplant.

A fully decorated one was quite expensive, so I made my own with a DIY kit.
It stands on my desk now, and it looks great.

## Why it's perfect for a desk

A mossarium is a small, mostly closed ecosystem. The water evaporates, condenses on the glass and drips back into the soil, so it needs very little water.
A well-balanced one can stay closed for months, or even years.
