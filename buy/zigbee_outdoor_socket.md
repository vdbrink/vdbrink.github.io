# Outdoor socket

[<< Best Buy Tips overview](smart_home_best_buy_tips#outdoor-sensors)

An outdoor Zigbee power socket makes it possible to automate everything you plug in outside, like garden lights, Christmas decorations or a pump.
These sockets are water-resistant (IP44), so they can handle the weather.
