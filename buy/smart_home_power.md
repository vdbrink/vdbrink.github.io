# Power adapters

[<< Best Buy Tips overview](smart_home_best_buy_tips#4-power-and-accessories)

Devices that run on USB power, like ESP boards, USB battery eliminators and smart USB adapters, need a power adapter.
A standard 5V USB adapter does the job for most of them.
