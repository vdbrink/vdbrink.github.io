# Soil sensor

[<< Best Buy Tips overview](smart_home_best_buy_tips#outdoor-sensors)

A soil sensor tells you if the plants in your garden have enough water.
With that you can send a notification, or even automatically start the irrigation when the soil becomes too dry.

## NAS-STH02B2

This sensor measures the soil temperature and humidity.\
It can be used for plants inside and outside.\
It contains a build-in battery that charges via a solar panel on top of it.

Do your plants have enough water?

Powered by two AAA batteries.
