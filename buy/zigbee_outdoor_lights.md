# Outdoor lights

[<< Best Buy Tips overview](smart_home_best_buy_tips#outdoor-sensors)

Outdoor lights make your garden cozy and safe, and with Zigbee you can control them from your automations, for example on sunset or when someone enters the garden.
These lights are water-resistant and suitable for outside: spotlights, floodlights and LED strips.

## Spotlight

A 7W / 24V DC spotlight with 16 million colors.
The brightness and color temperature are adjustable, and it's water-resistant.

Support 2.4G RF remote control directly.
